About Us

From one determined founder to a trusted nationwide team

Acuatec began in 2015 with a vision of delivering reliable, high-quality waterproofing. Its owner handled everything herself, from client relations, proposals and site inspections to billing, payroll and even material deliveries in her own car, which doubled as a mobile office.

Her hands-on approach earned a reputation for quality, and most early clients came through word-of-mouth referrals. Today Acuatec employs about 30 skilled professionals, works from a well-equipped office in Mandaluyong City, and handles projects nationwide. Technical consultants provide ongoing training for the team, and the company has been recognized with several awards for outstanding service.

Waterproofing Is a Must

Where water gets in

Waterproofing makes a structure resistant to water infiltration. It protects buildings and infrastructure from water damage, mold growth and structural deterioration, and can be applied during construction or as a repair for existing structures.

FAQ

Common questions

Is waterproofing only for new buildings?

No. It can be applied during construction or as a repair solution for existing structures.

How do you decide which system to use?

We inspect the location and site conditions first, then prescribe the method and materials suited to the area to be waterproofed.

Do you only do waterproofing?

We also offer flooring solutions (epoxy, polyurethane, rubberized coatings and concrete polishing), concrete crack injection repair, rectification works and painting works.

Do you work outside Metro Manila?

Yes, our projects are nationwide. Contact us with your location and we’ll discuss your site.

Do you offer after-sales service?

Yes. Exceptional after-sales service is a core part of how we work and one of our commitments to customers.
